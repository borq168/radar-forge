## Hot List Observations

**Agent‑first development** is the clearest theme today. Four of the top five trending repos by star count (superpowers, kilocode, GLM‑5, flue) are directly about building or improving agentic workflows. This aligns with the massive stars on established agent projects like hermes‑agent (197k) and AutoGPT (185k).

**Code intelligence is the breakout application**. codebase‑memory‑mcp’s huge single‑day spike (+2,322) points to a strong need for low‑latency, token‑efficient code understanding tools that integrate with agents and IDEs. Its claim of “99% fewer tokens” compared to naïve approaches is a key selling point.

**Vector databases remain hot**, but the interesting arrival is **zvec** – an in‑process, lightweight alternative to full‑fledged distributed vector stores. This suggests a growing segment for embedded AI retrieval.

On the model side, **timesfm** (time series foundation model) and **LTX‑2** (generative video) show that AI infrastructure is expanding beyond pure language. The community is actively exploring pre‑trained models for structured data and multimodal content.

Notably, no single LLM release or model weight drop appears to be driving today’s activity – the energy is around the **tooling and frameworks** that enable agents to operate more efficiently.
