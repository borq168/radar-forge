**[The post-quantum EO is an important milestone. Now it’s time to get to work](https://blog.cloudflare.com/post-quantum-eo-2026/)**
- Published: 2026-06-23

Cloudflare published a detailed analysis of Executive Order 14409, "Securing the Nation Against Advanced Cryptographic Attacks," signed by President Trump on June 22, 2026. Key deadlines: December 31, 2030 for federal agencies to transition most sensitive systems to post-quantum encryption; December 31, 2031 for post-quantum authentication; federal contractors must comply with post-quantum FIPS by end of 2030. Cloudflare welcomes the order, citing historical precedent of federal procurement driving adoption (IPv6, RPKI, DNSSEC). Notably, Cloudflare mentions that in April 2026 they moved their own full post-quantum security target to 2029, citing research breakthroughs from Google and Oratomic. The EO updates NIST’s 2024 guidance.
