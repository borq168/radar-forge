## 3. Hot List Observations
Today’s data reveals a massive concentration in the **AI Agents / Workflows** and **AI Infrastructure** categories, specifically focusing on "agent skills" and coding agent harnesses. Rather than new foundational models, the ecosystem is heavily optimizing how developers interact with existing coding agents (like Claude Code, Codex, and Cursor). Projects like `caveman` (token reduction), `ECC` (agent harness optimization), and `claude-mem` (persistent context) highlight a clear community push to reduce costs, improve context retention, and standardize agent behaviors. Furthermore, the **AI Applications** category shows strong momentum in vertical automation, with specialized agents for penetration testing (`strix`), video editing (`video-use`), and stock trading (`Vibe-Trading`) trending highly. This indicates a practical shift from general-purpose chatbots to highly specialized, task-specific autonomous workers.
