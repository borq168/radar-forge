## Hot List Observations

**Agent infrastructure dominates today's data.** The most striking pattern is the concentration of projects related to **AI agent skills, memory, and security**. The reverse-skill project (+2,297 stars) and TencentDB-Agent-Memory (+1,111 stars) both address the need for structured, reusable capabilities that agents can leverage across sessions. This aligns with the broader trend visible in topic search results, where agent frameworks (hermes-agent, CherryHQ, AutoGPT) and memory layers (mem0, cognee) continue to attract massive star counts.

**A new category emerges: PDF processing intelligence.** The pdf-inspector library (+2,540 stars) represents a specialized AI infrastructure tool — it's not just a PDF parser, but an intelligent router that distinguishes scanned vs. text-based PDFs. This points to growing demand for **document-aware AI pipelines** that handle heterogeneous data formats.

**Security and governance for AI agents is gaining traction.** Uber's ADR (+148 stars today) and the reverse-skill project both focus on making AI agents safer — one through observability and threat detection, the other through authorized penetration testing workflows. This suggests the community is maturing beyond "build fast" to "build securely."

**Lightweight inference remains a hot topic.** The airllm project (+1,711 stars) enabling 70B inference on a 4GB GPU, combined with picollm for on-device inference, shows sustained interest in democratizing access to large models without requiring expensive hardware.

**Education and onboarding projects are surging.** Microsoft's generative-ai-for-beginners (+783 today) and the datawhalechina hello-agents project (70,752 stars) indicate strong demand for structured learning paths in the AI agent space.
