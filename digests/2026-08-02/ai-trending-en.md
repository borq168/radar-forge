### 4. Community Hot Spots

- **Voice agents are surging** – Hugging Face’s speech-to-speech (+442) and voice-pro (+58) show that building local, real-time voice interfaces is a hot area. Developers should watch the underlying models (e.g., CosyVoice, F5-TTS) and the Gradio-based tooling.
- **Agent memory systems** – TencentDB-Agent-Memory and mem0ai (62k stars) are gaining traction. Persistent, shareable memory across agents and sessions is a key missing piece in production agent deployments.
- **Long-horizon agents** – ByteDance’s deer-flow (+209) tackles tasks that take minutes to hours, contrasting with single-turn agents. This direction is critical for real-world automation.
- **3D generation with structured latents** – Microsoft’s TRELLIS.2 (+107) represents a novel approach to 3D content creation. Developers interested in generative AI beyond 2D should explore its latent space design.
- **Local and privacy-first AI** – Projects like open-webui, ollama, and anything-llm continue to dominate the RAG/Infrastructure space, reflecting sustained demand for self-hosted, private AI solutions.
