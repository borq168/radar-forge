This suggests a maturation where agents are being equipped with domain-specific, structured knowledge rather than relying solely on general reasoning.

The `ai-agent` topic tag appears most frequently in the topic search results, spanning agent harnesses, memory systems, and application-specific agents.

A notable observation: **Apple Silicon optimization** appears in multiple projects (`omlx`, `tiny-llm`, `LabLLM`), reflecting the growing developer ecosystem around Apple's hardware for local AI inference and experimentation.
